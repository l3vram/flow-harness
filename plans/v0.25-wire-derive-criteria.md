# v0.25 — wire `deriveCriteria` into flow-run (requirement→evidence→done, automatic) — self-built

Closes the QA loop end-to-end: in objective mode, `flow-run` now turns the planner's free-text
`spec.acceptance` into executable QA criteria (`@flow/verify.deriveCriteria`) and attaches them to the
plan's final task, so the whole objective is QA-verified — with evidence and tickets — when the run
completes, without anyone writing criteria by hand.

## What
- `@flow/orchestrator` depends on `@flow/verify` (its node_modules symlink already exists from v0.24).
- New pure helper `attachAcceptanceCriteria(tasks, criteria): TaskSpec[]` — attaches the criteria to the
  last-listed task, returns a new list, never mutates the input; a no-op for empty tasks/criteria.
  (Multi-sink plans target the last-listed task — future work can split per sink.)
- `flow-run` (cli.ts), objective branch, after the plan is approved: when `config.deriveCriteria !== false`
  and `plan.spec.acceptance` is non-empty, `deriveCriteria(router, plan.spec.acceptance)` →
  `attachAcceptanceCriteria(taskList, criteria)`. Logs how many were derived and to which task.
- `RunConfig.deriveCriteria?: boolean` (default true) opts the behavior in/out.
